Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: July 27, 2026

At Blings N Luxe, we treat your privacy with the same meticulous care we apply to our software. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://blingsnluxe.site or use our mobile applications. Please read this policy carefully. By accessing our services, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Personal Data

  • Contact Information: Name, email address (e.g., when you reach out via admin@blingsnluxe.site).
  • Account Data: If you create an account within our apps, we collect username, preferences, and authentication credentials (securely hashed).

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device & Usage Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and referring URLs.
  • App Analytics: Crash logs, feature usage statistics, and performance data (anonymized where possible).
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: We use essential cookies for site functionality and optional analytics cookies (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our iOS applications and website.
  • Responding to your inquiries, support requests, or collaboration proposals.
  • Conducting internal analytics to enhance user experience and app stability.
  • Ensuring compliance with legal obligations and enforcing our terms of service.
  • Communicating important updates, security alerts, or policy changes (if you have opted in).

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses minimal cookies for operational purposes (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences). We may also use third-party analytics tools (like Google Analytics) that set cookies to report usage trends. These tools collect anonymized data and do not identify you personally.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of our site. For detailed options, visit Cookie Settings (coming soon).

4. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who assist with hosting, analytics, email delivery, or app distribution (bound by strict data processing agreements).
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or governmental request, we may disclose necessary information.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction (with prior notice).

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. These include encryption in transit (TLS 1.3), encrypted databases at rest, role-based access controls, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction & Objection: Limit or object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@blingsnluxe.site. We will respond within 30 days.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure adequate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place to protect your data privacy rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a prominent notice on our website or via email. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates the latest revision.
